Made for the browser · No install needed

How to Play

  1. Pick your fighter from the roster of 8 characters, then pick who they'll face.
  2. Battles are turn-based — each round, you and your opponent choose one move.
  3. Whoever has higher Speed acts first that round.
  4. Every fighter has 4 unique moves: some deal damage, some heal, some buff or debuff stats.
  5. Watch accuracy — powerful moves can miss. Critical hits deal bonus damage.
  6. Reduce your opponent's HP to zero before they do the same to you!
